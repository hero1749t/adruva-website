## 🚀 Weekly Link Building Planner (Suggested Workflow)

Ek sath saare links banana Google spam filters me a sakta hai. Isliye ye structured workflow apply karein:

- **Week 1:** Setup Google Business Profile, Crunchbase, and LinkedIn Page.
- **Week 2:** Create profiles on Clutch & GoodFirms (Complete them 100%).
- **Week 3:** List on IndiaMART, Justdial, and Sulekha (India Local SEO focus).
- **Week 4:** Create design profiles (GitHub, Behance, Dribbble) and start linking portfolios.
